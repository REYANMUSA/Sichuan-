The Story

Where heat
becomes art.

Sichuan cuisine is not simply about spice. It is about contrast — heat and freshness, fragrance and depth, texture and balance.

At Sichuan Chinese Restaurant, traditional techniques meet the ingredients and energy of Addis Ababa.
